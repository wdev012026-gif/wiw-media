# Privacy Policy – wiw-publisher

wiw-publisher is a private tool used only by the owner of the "What If It Wasn't" channel to upload the channel's own videos to its own YouTube and Instagram accounts.

- It does not collect, store or share data about anyone else.
- OAuth tokens are stored only in the owner's private environment (a local `.env` file and GitHub Secrets) and are used solely to upload videos.
- Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) or in Instagram settings → Apps and websites.

Contact: open an issue in this repository.
